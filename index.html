Chronogate

Chang Gate · Suzhou · A Time-Travel Web Experience

Why this project?

Chang Gate (阊门) has stood in Suzhou for over 2,200 years. It witnessed the rise and fall of dynasties, but most visitors walk past without feeling that depth.

Museum plaques and Wikipedia are static. Audio guides are passive. How do you make 2,000 years of history felt in seconds?

Our answer → A web app that turns time into a swipe. Six eras, one slider, and a visual sepia fade from past to present.

No existing heritage tool lets you drag a timeline from Tang Dynasty to 2024 and watch the world lose its sepia. Chronogate fills that gap.
