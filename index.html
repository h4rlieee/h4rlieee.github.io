Grand Vision

Enhancing the Chang Gate Experience

About the Application

Welcome to Grand Vision. This application is designed to revolutionize how visitors interact with the historic Chang gate. By bridging cutting-edge technology with cultural heritage, Grand Vision provides an immersive, easy-to-navigate, and deeply informative experience for everyone—from tech enthusiasts exploring the architecture to families enjoying a weekend getaway.

User Personas

Alex, The Tech-Savvy Explorer

Profile: A 28-year-old software engineer and history buff.

Goals: Alex visits the Chang gate specifically to experiment with the new technology surrounding it. They want to explore the intricate architectural details through augmented reality, test the limits of the app's interactive features, and dive deep into the technical execution of the historical reconstructions.

The Miller Family, Casual Travelers

Profile: A family of four on a weekend vacation looking for stress-free sightseeing.

Goals: The Millers want to enjoy the Chang gate without getting overwhelmed by dense historical plaques or confusing maps. They find the Grand Vision app incredibly helpful because it simplifies the history into engaging, bite-sized stories, offers clear digital wayfinding, and makes understanding the significance of the gate effortless for both the parents and the kids.
